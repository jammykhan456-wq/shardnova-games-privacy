1. About this policy

ShardNova Games (“we,” “our,” or “us”) publishes video games. This Privacy Policy describes our handling of information in ShardNova Games titles and related publisher support interactions. It includes Riftlume: Cascade Protocol and can apply to future games once the relevant game-specific disclosures have been added or updated.

Individual games may work differently. We provide a game-specific privacy notice for Riftlume. If a future title introduces accounts, advertising, analytics, cloud saves, multiplayer, or other services, we will update this policy and/or add that title's own notice before those features are introduced.

2. Game progress stored on your device

Some ShardNova Games titles store gameplay preferences, progress, achievements, unlocked content, and high scores locally on your device. Locally stored data normally remains on that device unless a game clearly offers a feature that sends it elsewhere.

In particular, Riftlume stores settings, saved runs, achievements, unlocks, records, and scores in local application/browser storage. The current Riftlume game does not require a ShardNova Games player account for its core gameplay. Read Riftlume's details.

You can normally remove locally stored game data by using a game-provided reset option (where available), clearing relevant browser/site data, or removing the game's saved application data. Doing so may permanently erase progress and achievements. Simply uninstalling a program does not always remove all application data.

3. Optional online and third-party functionality

Not all ShardNova Games titles use online services. If a game uses accounts, online leaderboards, remote/cloud saves, telemetry, advertising, purchases within the game, or social features, its game-specific notice will identify the data involved, its purposes, relevant recipients, and available controls. We will not treat future titles as automatically having the same privacy practices as Riftlume.

Depending on where you acquire or play a game, stores and platform providers (such as Microsoft Store or other distribution platforms) may separately handle downloads, orders, payment processing, crash information, device data, and their own service analytics under their policies.

4. Purchases and payments

Game purchases and refunds on third-party storefronts are handled through the applicable storefront. We do not process credit-card information directly through this privacy website. Storefronts may make purchase, transaction, or sales records available to publishers for fulfillment, accounting, customer service, fraud prevention, or legal obligations. Consult the store provider's terms and privacy notice for information it processes directly.

5. When you contact support

If you contact ShardNova Games, we may receive the email address or contact details you use, your message, troubleshooting information, and any screenshots or files that you voluntarily send. We use this information to respond, troubleshoot problems, investigate abuse or fraud where relevant, and comply with legal obligations.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, identity documents, or private game-save files unless specifically requested through an appropriate secure process. We keep support correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary for its purposes or applicable legal obligations and use relevant email/hosting providers to communicate with you.

6. Our privacy-policy website

This page is designed as a simple static website without our own advertising tags, analytics scripts, account system, or cookies. If hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may log visitors’ IP addresses for security and operate its hosting service under its own privacy statement. A different web host may process comparable service and security logs under its own terms.

7. When information may be shared

Game information is not automatically shared with ShardNova Games merely because it was saved locally. Information you choose to send to us, and any information provided by a storefront, may be handled by our service providers as required to deliver customer support, hosting, storefront fulfillment, security, or other relevant services. We may disclose information where legally required or necessary to protect users and our rights, subject to applicable law. We do not sell local Riftlume game saves to advertisers.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

  • Play Riftlume's core game without a ShardNova Games login.
  • Use in-game controls to manage preferences; clear local stored data if you wish, understanding that progress may be lost.
  • Contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information that we actually hold, subject to verification and applicable exceptions.
  • For information held solely by a storefront or hosting provider, use that provider's privacy controls and contact channels.

9. Children's privacy

Some of our games may be suitable for a wide age range, but age suitability does not mean a game collects personal information from children. Our present Riftlume game does not require players to supply personal information to begin core gameplay. Parents or guardians should assist children if they need to contact support. If we learn that we have collected information in a way inconsistent with applicable children's privacy laws, we will take appropriate action.

10. Policy changes

We may update this policy if our games, services, legal obligations, or data handling change. We will revise the effective date above and, where required, provide additional notice. Before a new title is released, its data practices should be reflected in this policy or in a linked game-specific notice.
